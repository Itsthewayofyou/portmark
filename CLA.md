# Portmark Contributor License Agreement

> **Draft, pending review by counsel.** This follows the structure of the widely used Apache
> Contributor License Agreements. It is not legal advice. The governing-law clause names Wisconsin;
> confirm that before relying on it.

Portmark uses Contributor License Agreements so that the rights in outside contributions are clear.
Sign **once** — it covers everything you contribute afterwards, and a contribution is not merged until
a signature is on record.

Two agreements follow. Sign the **Individual** one if you are contributing as yourself. Sign the
**Corporate** one if your employer owns the work, or if you are authorising contributions on behalf of
an organisation. An individual signature does not bind a company.

You keep the copyright in your own work. This is a licence, not an assignment.

## How to sign

A Contribution is not merged until a signature is on record.

1. **Electronically**, through the CLA check on your pull request, which records your identity, the
   agreement version, and the date; or
2. **On paper**, by signing below and sending a copy to the Project Owner. Use this route if you are
   signing for a legal entity and prefer a signed record.

```
Agreement signed:        [ ] Individual        [ ] Corporate

Full legal name:         ______________________________________

Entity (if Corporate):   ______________________________________

Title / authority:       ______________________________________

Repository identity:     ______________________________________   (e.g. GitHub username)

Email:                   ______________________________________

Signature:               ______________________________________

Date:                    ______________________________________

Corporate only — individuals authorised to Submit on the entity's behalf
(attach a schedule if more; keep it current with the Project Owner):

    ______________________________________________________________
```
