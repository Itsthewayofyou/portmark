## How to sign

A Contribution is not merged until a signature is on record.

1. **Sign electronically** through the CLA check on your pull request, which records your GitHub
   identity, the agreement version and the date; or
2. **Sign a copy** and send it to the Licensor, if you prefer a paper record or you are signing on
   behalf of a legal entity.

If you are contributing on behalf of your employer, sign as the entity — an individual signature does
not bind a company.
